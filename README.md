## ✨ About Project

DietDelish is a comprehensive web application designed to help users achieve their health and wellness goals through personalized meal planning, nutritional tracking, and delicious recipe recommendations.

- 🥗 **Personalized Meal Plans:** Tailor your meals to your dietary needs and preferences.
- 🍲 **Recipe Discovery:** Get recipe suggestions based on your goals and available ingredients.
- 📊 **Nutrition Tracker:** Monitor calories, macros, and key nutrients.
- 📈 **Progress Monitoring:** Visualize your journey with interactive charts and summaries.
- 💡 **User-Friendly:** Enjoy a seamless and intuitive interface.

---

## 🚀 Purpose

DietDelish was built to make healthy eating accessible and enjoyable for everyone. Whether you want to lose weight, gain muscle, or simply maintain a balanced diet, DietDelish is your personal companion on the path to better health.
